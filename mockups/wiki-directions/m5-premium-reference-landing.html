About This Wiki

The Climate Finance Wiki is a curated reference built for communications professionals. It covers the terminology, institutions, instruments, and debates that define climate finance — explained in plain English, not financial jargon. Every article is written from a comms perspective: what do you need to know to brief your team, write accurate copy, or spot a misleading claim?
