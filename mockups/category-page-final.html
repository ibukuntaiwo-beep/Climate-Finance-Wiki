Quick Glossary

Greenium
Lower borrowing cost for green bond issuers — the market premium for credible climate labelling.
Additionality
The reduction must be additional to what would have happened anyway. Without it, an offset is just accounting fiction.
Step-up
Coupon increase triggered when an SLB issuer misses its sustainability performance targets.
