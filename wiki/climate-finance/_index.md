# Climate Finance — Index

> The mobilization of capital to address climate change: reducing emissions, adapting to its effects, and redirecting financial flows away from high-carbon activities.

Climate finance sits at the intersection of environmental policy, macroeconomics, and capital markets. Understanding it requires knowing both the climate context and the financial plumbing that moves money through the global economy. This wiki is written for communications professionals — the goal is fluency, not specialist expertise.

Start anywhere. Every article links to others.
