# CLAUDE.md

Guidance for **Claude Code** in the Climate Finance Wiki project. For OpenAI Codex, see `AGENTS.md`.

## What This Project Is

The Climate Finance Wiki is a public hobby website for communications professionals. It includes the Astro site, wiki article source, raw source material, and promotional video folders.

## Core Rules

- Plain English first. Avoid academic or financial-model jargon unless explicitly defined.
- Every content decision must pass: "Would a comms professional need to know this to do their job well?"
- Use `[[wikilinks]]` in wiki article source.
- Treat `UBIQUITOUS_LANGUAGE.md` as the canonical climate finance glossary.
- Respect the curation gate before creating new articles from raw material.
- Do not commit dependency folders, build outputs, caches, or rendered video exports unless the owner explicitly asks.
